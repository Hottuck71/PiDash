Copy pictures into this folder and PiDash shows them as a slideshow behind
the calendar and weather. Videos go in the "videos" folder. With nothing in
either folder, the background is dark.

- JPG, PNG, WEBP, GIF and BMP work. iPhone HEIC photos do not; save those as
  JPG first.
- Photos and videos share about 400 MB (roughly 100 phone photos).
- Turn the Pi off before taking the SD card out.
- PHOTO_SECONDS in dashboard-settings.txt sets how long each picture stays up.
