Copy videos into this folder and PiDash plays them in the slideshow, between
the pictures from the "photos" folder, behind the calendar and weather.

- MP4, MOV and most others work. They play without sound.
- The Pi makes a small copy of each new video first, which takes a few
  minutes (4K: much longer). Your file is not changed.
- Photos and videos share about 400 MB: roughly 3 minutes of phone video.
  Short clips saved from a chat are much smaller.
- Turn the Pi off before taking the SD card out.
- VIDEO_SECONDS in dashboard-settings.txt sets how long a video may play.
